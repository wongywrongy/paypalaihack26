# Product imagery provenance

`public/assets/dalbit-products.png` was generated with the built-in image tool on 2026-10-22. The one sheet supplies four catalog crops through CSS positioning; it is not sliced or repainted. It was inspected at full size: amber white-pump essence, amber black-cap old essence, frosted rice toner, sage cica mask box and sachet. Only the fictional Dalbit name appears.

The exact prompt is embedded in the PNG and reproduced below. No real product photography or stock photograph is represented as a Dalbit product. The mockup SVG illustrations were not shipped.

```text
Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: one high resolution square product catalog contact sheet for the fictional Korean skincare brand Dalbit. Four equal square quadrants, clean 2 by 2 grid, absolutely no visible dividers. Each quadrant contains ONE product centered with generous padding; product silhouette fully inside its quadrant. Top left: amber glass 100 ml snail essence bottle with WHITE pump, ivory label reading only 'Dalbit'. Top right: same amber essence in OLD packaging with BLACK screw cap and dark charcoal label reading only 'Dalbit'. Bottom left: frosted ivory cylindrical 150 ml rice toner bottle with champagne cap, ivory label reading only 'Dalbit'. Bottom right: soft sage green paper rectangular box of cica sheet masks, one matching sheet-mask sachet beside it, text only 'Dalbit'. Product photography, highly realistic materials and soft studio light from upper left, warm off-white seamless backgrounds, gentle realistic contact shadows, camera at bottle height, consistent size and scale. No graphics, no leaves, no decorative props, no extra words, no real brands, no watermarks, no labels outside the packs. Deliver one square image with four clearly separated product shots. Do not include any UI.
```

The two bundled fonts and their OFL files are in `public/fonts/`; pinned source hashes and license attribution are in the root third-party notice and its shipped copy at `public/assets/dependency-notices.txt`.
