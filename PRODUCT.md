# Coalition
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
React, TypeScript, Vite, selected actual Magic UI components; FastAPI; PostgreSQL; one durable-job worker; configurable structured LLM adapter; PayPal sandbox adapter. Render deployment configuration is provided.
## Users
Shoppers seeking compatible headphones within a maximum delivered total and arrival deadline; hackathon judges; operators preparing disclosed sandbox buyer sessions.
## Product Purpose
Turn editable shopping requirements into evidence-backed eligibility, a bounded two-role negotiation, an immutable merchant-authorized group quote, and a buyer-owned persistent checkout and receipt.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Six real headphone models with simulated merchant offers. One unit per buyer, USD, included simulated shipping, explicitly zero simulated tax, one configured sandbox business payee. Only new profile: capacity five, minimum three. Actual prices come from three merchant policies; one observed five-person example is $89 at three and $85 at five. Counts represent actual verified commitments in connected mode; fixture counts are explicitly simulated. Unknown mandatory requirements block eligibility. Quote and buyer consent remain fixed once approval begins. Settlement freezes membership and price at the accepted deadline; all selected payments complete or captures are refunded and remaining authorizations unwound. Unresolved financial records survive new runs. Live models cannot approve payment.
## Brand Commitments
Coalition is a storefront with intelligent search and group purchasing. Products and photographs lead; operational details appear only when useful to the shopper. Commonplace Audio is a fictional merchant; settlement uses one sandbox business account. White and pale neutral surfaces, fine borders, Archivo text, tabular numbers, solid indigo actions. No decorative agent avatars or speculative payment animation.
## Evidence on Hand
Existing payment foundation and isolated PostgreSQL recovery checks; request-to-negotiation-to-lower-capture fixture checks on desktop and mobile. GPU model and structured live evaluation are recorded separately from fixture and provider verification in VERIFICATION.md. Screenshots live under `.impeccable/review/`. Genuine sandbox lower capture, void, refund, webhook and remaining-hold behavior require provider evidence; fixture results do not establish them.
## Product Principles
Explain eligibility with sources and uncertainty; enforce merchant pricing authority and inventory in code; reserve inventory atomically; persist immutable terms and intended payment operations; distinguish approval, authorization, capture and refund confirmation. A full authorization count alone never means purchase success.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Native editable form controls, visible focus, keyboard access, explicit text states, responsive offer and action hierarchy, reduced motion, stable monetary amounts. Refresh restores owned state without replaying success celebrations. Pricing help remains an optional link after payment controls.
## Delegated Decisions
Preserve Archivo, pale neutral surfaces and indigo actions while following the user’s storefront composition: browse products before search, concise selected-deal panel, collapsed requirements and negotiation history, compact demo badge, owned My purchases. Reuse the existing payment foundation. Prefer the five-buyer live profile. The previous multi-category storefront, demo bag, optional checkout assistant and new legacy/large preparation are retired. Recover historical obligations through owned checkout/operator controls; construct historical regression cases only in test fixtures. Self-hosted models remain server-side and fail closed when unavailable.
