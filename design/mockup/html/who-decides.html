Who decides what

LLM · language and ideas SOLVER · math CODE · authority PAYPAL · money
Step Done by Guarantee
Turn a person’s words into items, rules and a max LLM Every field ties back to their words; at most one question. Can’t set prices or charge anyone.
Approve the max as a PayPal hold PAYPAL Orders v2 authorization for the max. The max is also enforced in code, because PayPal lets a capture exceed a hold (up to 115% or +$75).
Assign people to variants and split shared costs SOLVER Respects every rule and max. One price per item per order, so stating a higher max never raises your price.
Pick the next offer and suggest new terms LLM Ideas only. The solver re-checks every proposal before it is sent.
Read the other side’s message LLM Parsed into a fixed offer format and treated as data, so “ignore your rules” changes nothing.
Score offers for the shop CODE Lowest prices live in code. The shop’s language model sees scores, never the floor.
Accept or reject CODE Accept only if everyone is under their max and the group beats its threshold. The language model never accepts.
Charge, release, pay the shop, track PAYPAL Capture each share at deal close; void every hold if no deal; pay the shop once it ships; add tracking to each payment.
